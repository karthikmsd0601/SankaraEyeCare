The Challenge

Teams across Sankara Eye Care spend much of their day on knowledge work. Preparing recurring reports took around four hours, and finding information in the Trust’s internal policy documents took more than five hours. Sankara Eye Care wanted to optimise these processes with AI in a responsible, repeatable and measurable way, and to bring it into daily use.

The Solution

Quadra’s Accelerated Intelligence Group worked with Sankara Eye Care to move Claude into live use. Quadra ran hands-on enablement sessions and use-case workshops, helped identify the processes to optimise, and supported the move into daily use.

Since October 2026, five users across the IT, Operations and Customer Care teams have used Claude and Claude Cowork on the Claude Team plan in their daily work. They use it to:

01

Retrieve information from internal policy documents

Claude finds and summarises the answers teams need from the Trust’s internal policy documents.

02

Work with Microsoft 365

Claude is connected to the Trust’s Microsoft 365 services through an MCP server, so it works with the organisation’s own information.

03

Consolidate information into reports

AI brings details from these sources together, which speeds up report preparation.
